# Results, 21 November 2026

Machine: Intel UHD 630 (Mesa 26.0.8, Vulkan), Hyprland, monitor scale 1.6,
window 1600×1000 logical, hole 1600×920 logical (3200×1840 buffer at scale 2).
CPU over 10 s after a 6 s warm-up, 100% = one core. "App total" is the whole
probe process (main, render and WebKit's UI-side threads); "web" is the
WebKitWebProcess.

| Scenario | fps | Main thread | Render thread | App total | Web process | App + web |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas, full frame (today's path) | 57 | 34% | – | 36% | 48% | **84%** |
| Subsurface, full frame upload | 61 | 1% | 26% (4.0–4.5 ms/frame) | 28% | 0% | **28%** |
| Subsurface, 64-row strips | 60 | 1% | 4% (0.6 ms/frame) | 6% | 1% | **7%** |
| Subsurface, clear only | 61 | – | 0.3 ms/frame | – | – | – |

Findings:

1. **It works on GTK 3 / WebKitGTK / Hyprland.** The subsurface below the
   transparent window shows through the page's hole, the HTML overlay draws
   above it, and the web view keeps all input. No Wayland protocol error
   (issue #1's error 71 came from drawing into GTK's own surface) and no
   GTK warnings in any run.
2. **The main thread is free.** Remote frames no longer touch it: 1% instead of
   34% here (about 80% in the real app at 40 fps on 20 November). That is the
   thread that carries input.
3. **CPU for the same full-frame stream drops by about two thirds** (84% to
   28%), and what remains is the frame upload on its own thread. Real RDP
   updates are dirty rectangles; the strip case (7%) is closer to terminal
   work, an order of magnitude below today's path.
4. The canvas case here excludes 1-1's frame IPC to the web view
   (`ReceiveQueue` was 20% of samples in the real app on 21 November), so the
   real gain is larger than this table shows.

Open for the design (not tested here):

- Resizing and moving: the position follows `queue_draw`; check during
  interactive resize, sidebar toggles and tab switches.
- Fractional scale: buffer scale 2, downscaled by the compositor. The
  `wp_fractional_scale` protocol and `wp_viewporter` would give exact pixels.
- Several sessions (one subsurface per visible tab, or one shared and
  switched), and hiding it when the session view is not shown.
- XWayland (`GDK_BACKEND=x11`) and X11 sessions need a fallback (the canvas).
- Other compositors (GNOME, KDE) before shipping.
